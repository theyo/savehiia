YOUR VOICE MATTERS
PROTECT TEACHER‑DIRECTED LEARNING IN HIIA
Please take a moment to email TVUSD leadership and tell them how the teacher directed program has supported your child.
Even if your plans are changing next year, your story helps ensure this valuable learning option remains available for families who rely on it.
Send your message today. Your perspective could help protect an essential educational pathway for current and future TVUSD students.

Tips for writing your email

  • Send an email to each board member and district leader directly (not one email to all)
  • Introduce yourself and your child(ren)
  • Share specific examples of how teacher-directed learning has benefited your child
  • Mention any challenges you have faced and how the program has helped overcome them
  • Express your concerns about the shocking announcement that the program is being discontinued without community input or consideration
  • Express your hope that TVUSD will continue to support and invest in teacher-directed learning options like HIIA
